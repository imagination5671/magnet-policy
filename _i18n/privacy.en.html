Sangsanglyeog (the “Company”) protects the personal information of people who use the Magnet app (the “Service”) in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법) of the Republic of Korea. This policy explains what information the Company receives and why, how long it keeps it, and how it protects it.

2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following information is stored only on the user’s device and is not sent to the Company’s servers. It is deleted when you delete the app or delete your account in the app.

  • Records: goals, affirmations, practice records, journal entries, dream photos, display settings
  • Answers on the introductory screens: year of birth (and the age calculated from it), whether you have a dream you want to achieve, the kinds of things you want to have, whether you know the law of attraction, how often you think about your dreams, whether you have ever written down your dreams, your biggest obstacle, and what you have tried so far
  • My Dreams: the house you chose, your car (the color you chose and the name you gave it), the things you want to have

Your answers on the introductory screens and My Dream are used only to tailor the remaining-time calculation, personalized plan, payment screen wording and personalized notification text to you. Because the Company does not receive this information, it does not use it elsewhere or provide it to anyone. You do not have to answer the year of birth question, and you can change or delete your answers (they are deleted when you delete the app).

If you use the home screen widget, your dream photo and your biggest goal sentence are copied to widget-only storage on the same device. This information also never leaves the device.

Device Permissions

  • Microphone and speech recognition: used to count how many times you say your affirmations aloud. Processing is done on the device whenever possible, and recordings are not stored. If the device does not support on-device recognition, Apple’s speech recognition service (on iPhone) or the device’s speech recognition service, such as Google’s (on Android), may process it.
  • Photos: used only when you choose dream photos, room photos, profile photos, post photos or asset verification documents. The app accesses only the photos you choose.
  • Notifications: used to send motivation, dream reminder and practice notifications. Notification text is created and scheduled on the device using the on-device information above (name, biggest goal, affirmations, My Dream, etc.) and does not pass through the Company’s servers. We do not send promotional notifications.

You can turn off permissions at any time in your phone’s settings (iPhone Settings, or on Android, Settings → Apps → Magnet → Permissions), and even if you turn them off, you can continue to use everything except the related feature.

3. Retention Period and Destruction

  • Member information, Lounge records and files uploaded to the Company’s servers (profile photos, room photos, post photos, and asset verification documents and videos under review) are deleted immediately when you withdraw (“Delete Account” in the app).
  • Original asset verification materials (document photos, recorded videos, name, date of birth, balance) are deleted within 7 days after the review is completed. After that, only the tier, document type and review date are kept, and these are deleted when the badge’s validity period (1 year) expires or when you withdraw.
  • Information that laws require to be retained is kept separately for the required period. For example, under the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (전자상거래 등에서의 소비자보호에 관한 법률): records of payment and supply of goods, 5 years; records of consumer complaints and dispute handling, 3 years.
  • Electronic files are deleted using methods that make them unrecoverable, and we do not receive paper documents.

4. Provision to Third Parties

The Company does not provide users’ personal information to others, except where the user has consented or where laws have special provisions.

6. Users’ Rights

  • You may at any time request to view, correct or delete your information, or to stop its processing.
  • You can change your nickname and profile photo directly in the app, and if you withdraw through More → Delete Account in the app, your information on the server is deleted.
  • For other requests, please send an email to the address below, and we will inform you of the result within 10 days.

7. How We Keep Information Safe

  • All communications are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The database has access rules so that each user can see only their own information.
  • Login information is stored in the device’s Keychain (iPhone) or in the app’s private storage (Android).
  • Asset verification documents are kept in private storage and can be viewed only by the reviewers.

8. Automatic Collection Tools and Advertising

The Service does not use ad tracking, advertising identifiers or cookies. It does not track your activity in other apps or websites.

9. Children Under 14

Only people aged 14 or older may sign up for the Service. If we learn that a person under 14 has signed up, we will delete that information.
